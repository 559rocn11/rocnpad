Privacy Policy — ROCN Pad

Effective date: November 19, 2026
Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy covers ROCN Pad (rocnpad.559rocn.workers.dev), a personal knowledge app operated by Roc, doing business as ROCN / ReelyROCN, a sole proprietor based in Fresno, California.

Plain version: we collect as little as we can, we do not sell your information, and we do not use advertising trackers. This page explains exactly what we do collect, why, and who else touches it.

1. Information we collect

  • When you sign in with Google: we receive your Google profile information (name, email address, and profile picture) and store it, along with your activity in the app, in our database (Supabase). We use it only to run your account and your data in the app. You can sign out at any time.
  • The content you enter in the app: your notes and knowledge entries. That content is yours, and we use it only to provide the app to you.
  • App activity: we keep a small log of actions you take in the app (for example, signing in or saving an entry) so the app works and so we can fix bugs. The log records what kind of action happened, not the contents of your notes.

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect only to:

  • Let you sign in and use your account.
  • Store and show you the content you entered.
  • Reply to your messages.
  • Fix bugs and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for advertising. We do not use it to build profiles about you.

3. Third parties that receive information

ROCN Pad relies on two services. When you use the app, those services receive some of your information directly — usually your IP address, browser information, and whatever you enter into the app:

  • Supabase — stores your account profile, your app activity log, and your app data.
  • Google — sign-in (Google OAuth) and fonts.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We are not responsible for how they handle information once it reaches them.

4. Cookies and local storage

Our own servers do not set first-party cookies. The app may use your browser's local storage to save settings and keep you signed in. That information stays on your device. Third-party embeds (such as Google Fonts) may set their own cookies when they load. You can clear cookies and local storage at any time in your browser settings.

5. How long we keep your information

We keep your information only as long as it is needed to run your account and the app. If you ask us to delete your information (see below), we will delete what we can, except for records we are legally required to keep.

6. Your choices — access, correction, and deletion

You can ask us to show you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. To make a request, email 559rocn@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy request" and tell us what you need. We will respond within a reasonable time.

If you have a ROCN Pad account, you can also sign out at any time. To delete your account and your data, use Settings → "Your Data Is Yours" → Delete My Account in the app — or see our account deletion page. You can also email us and we will delete your account data manually.

7. Children's privacy

ROCN Pad is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Do Not Track

Our sites do not currently respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals, because we do not track you across websites in the first place. We run no advertising trackers, no analytics pixels, and no session-replay tools.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and post the new version here. If a change affects how we handle information you have already given us, we will say so in the updated policy. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or about your information, contact us at 559rocn@gmail.com.

A note on scope: This policy is written for a small sole-proprietor business in California. It is not a substitute for legal advice. If you have questions about your rights under California law, contact the California Attorney General's office or a California attorney.
