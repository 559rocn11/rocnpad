Delete Your ROCN Pad Account

Last updated: November 19, 2026

You can delete your ROCN Pad account and your data at any time. This page explains how, and what happens when you do.

How to delete your account

Your data is yours — delete it or take it with you in one click, right in the app. In ROCN Pad, go to Settings → "Your Data Is Yours" → Delete My Account:

  1. Type DELETE to confirm you mean it.
  2. We email a 6-digit code to your Google account address. Enter it in the app. ROCN Pad signs in with Google only — there is no password — so the emailed code is how we check it is really you.
  3. Confirm one last time. Your account is deleted and you are signed out.

If you would rather not use the app, deletion by email still works: email 559rocn@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my ROCN Pad account" and the email address you signed in with. We will delete your account and reply to confirm once it is done. We will respond within a reasonable time.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile — your name, email address, and profile picture from Google sign-in.
  • Your app activity log.
  • The content you entered in the app — your notes, projects, settings, and recaps. Your content lives in your browser only; we never see it. Deleting your account wipes it from your browser as well.

What happens after deletion

  • Your account stops working. You will not be able to sign in to ROCN Pad with that account.
  • Deletion is permanent. If you sign in again later with the same Google account, a new empty account will be created — your old notes and data will not come back.
  • We keep nothing of yours except records we are legally required to keep, if any.

Want a copy of your data first?

Before you delete, take your data with you in one click. In the app, go to Settings → "Your Data Is Yours" → Download My Data. You get one JSON file with your profile, your activity log, and all your content — notes, projects, and settings. Keep that file private: it contains your saved API keys.

You can also ask for a copy by email: email 559rocn@gmail.com with the subject line "Export my ROCN Pad data" and the email address you signed in with. We will send you a copy of your profile, your activity log, and the content you entered.

Signing out is not deleting. Signing out of ROCN Pad only ends your session — your account and data stay. To remove them, use Settings → "Your Data Is Yours" → Delete My Account, or the email request above.
